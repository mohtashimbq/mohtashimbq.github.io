Profile

A concise view of my academic path, applied work and the kinds of problems I tend to return to.

In brief

I am a lecturer and researcher in Sydney, with a background spanning telecommunications engineering, computing, image and signal processing, and applied AI.

Research and teaching, informed by engineering.

I currently work in the School of IT & Engineering at Melbourne Institute of Technology. My role includes teaching, research supervision, unit coordination and curriculum work. Alongside academia, I have worked in computer vision engineering, where the focus was less on demonstrations and more on dependable data, model selection, optimisation and training workflows.

Earlier research at Sunway University centred on image quality assessment and enhancement, with work involving permutation entropy, perceptual image metrics and machine learning. That thread continues to shape how I think about AI: representation matters, evaluation matters, and a model is only as useful as the assumptions around it.

I keep this site intentionally selective. Detailed records sit in the CV; these pages are meant to make the work easier to understand.
